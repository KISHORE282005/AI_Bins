Bin Recommendation Rules

A category applies only when the demand volume and the demand weight are both inside its range

Bounds are inclusive at both ends. Where two weight bands meet (10, 15, 25, 40 Kg) more than one category can qualify; the smallest suitable category wins and Priority breaks any remaining tie, so the result is always deterministic.
